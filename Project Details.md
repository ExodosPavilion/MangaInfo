# MangaInfo.xml

Think of the ComicInfo.xml format but more specific to the type of metadata manga or manhwa or manhua store.

## Requirements Gathering

First we should figure out what fields should even be possible for this new xml format to have.
Would it be possible to check on the following metadata sites to see what they offer for the manga/manhwa/manhua:
- MangaBaka
- MyAnimeList
- AniList
- Kitsu
- MangaUpdates
- Shikimori
- Bangumi
- Hikka
- Comick

Would also like to support 18+ content as well such as hentai or doujin or pornhwa, but I don't know of any metadata providers for that kind of content.

Also look into what the mylar schema supports. I know its more of a top level series metadata scheme but maybe it would be a good idea to look into creating a custom schema for that as well.
